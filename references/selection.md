# Choosing photos of people

People forgive soft focus; they do not forgive looking bad. Review candidates at ~900 px, one burst per sheet,
and pick like a friend would.

Reject or replace:
- eyes closed, half-blink, squint, grimace mid-word, mouth full / mid-chew, licking, mid-bite
- shot from below (chin/nostrils, belly), wide-angle face distortion at the frame edge, nose-in-lens selfies
- one person cropped through the face or out of frame, hand in hair covering the face
- harsh backlight making faces black, night photos with blur or orange noise
- food photos where the person is a side note or the table is messy (dirty plates, napkins) — prefer the
  person's moment or a clean dish
- duplicates: keep the single best of a burst; in a mosaic, use different moments (laugh, kiss, look)

Keep: genuine laughs, both people looking good, scenery + people balanced, the funny moment the person mentions
(ask before using a joke shot of someone).

When the person rejects a photo, ask yourself why and apply it to similar shots elsewhere in the film, then list
what else you removed for the same reason.
